# 🎮 Virtual Mouse: Simple Step-by-Step User Guide

Welcome! This guide explains how to run and use your Virtual Mouse in plain, simple language.

---

## ⚡ Quick Start (Only 2 Steps!)

### Step 1: Open Terminal
Open **PowerShell** or **Command Prompt** on your computer.

### Step 2: Run the Program
Copy and paste this command, then press **Enter**:

```powershell
cd "d:\6 Months - SDE2 level\python-ai-vision\01-virtual-mouse"
python main.py
```

A camera window titled **"Virtual Mouse - Cyberpunk Edition"** will open on your screen.

---

## 🖐️ How to Control Your Mouse with Hand Gestures

Hold your hand in front of your webcam (about 1.5 to 2.5 feet away). Keep your palm facing the camera.

### 1. 🖱️ Move the Cursor
* **What to do:** Point your **index finger** up and move it around.
* **What you see:** A glowing green dot follows your fingertip, leaving a cool neon light trail behind it. Your computer's real mouse arrow will move across your desktop!
* **Tip:** Keep your finger inside the glowing cyan corner box (**Active Control Zone**) to easily reach all 4 corners of your screen.

---

### 2. 👆 Left Click (Normal Click)
* **What to do:** Quickly **tap your thumb and index finger together** (like a gentle pinch) and let go immediately.
* **What you see:** A yellow ring expands outward (shockwave pulse) and you will hear a subtle click sound. Your computer registers a left click!

---

### 3. ✌️ Double Click (Open folders/files)
* **What to do:** Quickly **pinch your thumb and index finger twice** in a row (tap-tap!).
* **What you see:** Double yellow shockwaves pulse, opening the file or folder under your cursor.

---

### 4. ✊ Drag and Drop (Move windows or files)
* **What to do:** **Pinch your thumb and index finger together and HOLD them together**.
* **What you see:** The mode changes to **"DRAGGING"** in red. Now move your hand to drag the window or file!
* **To Drop:** Simply **open your fingers** to let go.

---

### 5. 🖱️ Right Click (Open Context Menus)
* **What to do:** Pinch your **thumb and middle finger** together.
* **What you see:** A glowing magenta pulse appears, and the right-click menu opens!

---

### 6. 📜 Scroll Up or Down (Browse web pages)
* **What to do:** Hold both your **index and middle fingers up** side-by-side (like a peace sign ✌️).
* **What to do next:** Move your hand **up** to scroll up, or **down** to scroll down.

---

## 🛑 How to Stop / Close the Program

Whenever you want to stop:
* Click on the camera window.
* Press the **`q`** key or **`ESC`** key on your keyboard.
* The webcam and program will close safely.

---

## 💡 3 Easy Tips for Best Performance
1. **Good Lighting:** Make sure your room has decent lighting so your webcam clearly sees your fingers.
2. **Clear Background:** Avoid having another person standing directly behind your hand.
3. **Natural Distance:** Sit comfortably in your normal chair; you don't need to stretch your arm close to the camera.
